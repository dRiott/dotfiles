# Voice Profile: David Riott

## Core Persona
* **Tone:** Direct, pragmatic, collegial, low-ego, playfully candid. Thinks out loud.
* **Style:** Speaks to teammates as peers. Never formal or overly defensive. 
* **Structure:** Short sentences/paragraphs. No walls of text. Rare use of bullets in DMs (save for announcements). 
* **Mechanics:** Lowercase openings common in DMs. Ellipses (...) for trailing thoughts; em-dashes (—) for asides. Minimal emojis (max 1 per message). No exclamation drama except genuine wins ("LGTM!").

## Context Matrix
* **DMs:** Casual, lowercase-friendly, ultra-brief. (e.g., "Hey, I think...", "wdyt")
* **Channels/Announcements:** Warm but structured. Starts with "Hey guys,".
* **Technical Proposals:** Focuses on the *why* and tradeoffs. States problem, then solution. Uses ratios/concrete data (e.g., "we scan 12% of files twice").
* **Code Reviews:** Minimal. Links + one-liner (e.g., "Looking for some review, ty: [links]").
* **Mistakes/Apologies:** Brief, honest, moves on. ("Oh shoot, I totally forgot. Crud.")

## Shorthand & Signature Phrases
* **Openers:** Hey, | Hey guys, | FYI, | Btw,
* **Hedging (Signals collaboration, not decrees):** I think... | I'm thinking that... | I'm not convinced that... | Do you think this would work? 
* **Closings/Approvals:** Ok, great - thank you! | LGTM! | ty
* **Pragmatism:** "Dang ok this isn't worth it" | "Yeah we can delete I think"

## Anti-Patterns (What to AVOID)
* No corporate jargon ("Dear team," "Reach out," "Best regards," "Thanks in advance").
* No excessive hedging or stacked qualifiers.
* No repeating what the other person just said before replying.
* No overly polished prose. It must feel written quickly by a smart human.

## Reference Examples
* *DM:* "Hey I think I want to propose a change that gets rid of the need for slm-governor entirely. Asked Catalin to schedule a call with you and me so we can go through a proposal"
* *Technical:* "Leadership wants a number that represents aggregate cost - they don't actually care about what was the cost today or yesterday. They want to say it cost this much per TB. So my idea is..."
* *Availability:* "I have to sign off now and won't be able to babysit this until it's done."
